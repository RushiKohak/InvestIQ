\# User Stories — Investment Discovery Experience



\## Epic 1 — Investment Discovery



\### US-01 — Search Investment Products



\*\*As an investor,\*\*  

I want to search for investment products,  

\*\*so that\*\* I can quickly find products relevant to my interests.



\*\*Acceptance Criteria\*\*

\- User can enter a search query.

\- Matching products are displayed.

\- Search results update based on the query.

\- Empty-state messaging is displayed when no results are found.



\---



\### US-02 — Filter Investment Products



\*\*As an investor,\*\*  

I want to filter investment products,  

\*\*so that\*\* I can narrow down the available options.



\*\*Acceptance Criteria\*\*

\- User can apply supported filters.

\- Multiple filters can be applied.

\- Active filters are visible.

\- User can clear individual filters.

\- User can clear all filters.



\---



\## Epic 2 — Fund Information



\### US-03 — View Fund Details



\*\*As an investor,\*\*  

I want to view important information about a fund,  

\*\*so that\*\* I can understand the product before selecting it.



\*\*Acceptance Criteria\*\*

\- Fund category is displayed.

\- Historical performance information is displayed.

\- Risk information is clearly presented.

\- Relevant fund characteristics are displayed.

\- Investment minimum is visible where applicable.



\---



\### US-04 — Understand Risk Information



\*\*As an investor,\*\*  

I want understandable risk information,  

\*\*so that\*\* I can evaluate the characteristics of an investment product.



\*\*Acceptance Criteria\*\*

\- Risk information is clearly visible.

\- Technical terminology is explained where appropriate.

\- Risk information does not make unsupported personalized recommendations.



\---



\## Epic 3 — Fund Comparison



\### US-05 — Compare Funds



\*\*As an investor,\*\*  

I want to compare multiple funds,  

\*\*so that\*\* I can evaluate alternatives before making a selection.



\*\*Acceptance Criteria\*\*

\- User can select multiple eligible funds.

\- Key attributes are shown in a consistent format.

\- Comparison can be exited easily.

\- User can proceed from comparison to a fund-details or selection flow.



\---



\## Epic 4 — Investment Flow



\### US-06 — Select a Fund



\*\*As an investor,\*\*  

I want to select a fund after reviewing its information,  

\*\*so that\*\* I can begin the investment process.



\*\*Acceptance Criteria\*\*

\- Selection action is clearly visible.

\- Selected fund is correctly identified.

\- User is taken to the investment flow.



\---



\### US-07 — Enter Investment Amount



\*\*As an investor,\*\*  

I want to enter my investment amount,  

\*\*so that\*\* I can proceed with the transaction.



\*\*Acceptance Criteria\*\*

\- User can enter an amount.

\- Invalid amounts are rejected with clear messaging.

\- Applicable minimum investment requirements are shown.

\- User can review the selected product before continuing.



\---



\### US-08 — Complete Investment



\*\*As an investor,\*\*  

I want a clear transaction flow,  

\*\*so that\*\* I can complete my investment without unnecessary friction.



\*\*Acceptance Criteria\*\*

\- User can review the transaction before confirmation.

\- Payment status is clearly communicated.

\- Successful investments display confirmation.

\- Failed transactions display actionable error information.



\---



\## Epic 5 — Analytics



\### US-09 — Track Fund Discovery



\*\*As a product analyst,\*\*  

I want to track fund discovery interactions,  

\*\*so that\*\* I can understand where users engage or drop off.



\*\*Acceptance Criteria\*\*

\- Fund searches are tracked.

\- Filter usage is tracked.

\- Fund-detail views are tracked.

\- Fund comparisons are tracked.

\- Fund selections are tracked.



\---



\### US-10 — Measure Conversion



\*\*As a product analyst,\*\*  

I want to measure conversion between investment funnel stages,  

\*\*so that\*\* product teams can identify areas requiring investigation.



\*\*Acceptance Criteria\*\*

\- Funnel stages are consistently defined.

\- Stage-level conversion can be calculated.

\- Conversion can be segmented by relevant dimensions such as acquisition channel.

\- Metrics can be compared over time.



\---
